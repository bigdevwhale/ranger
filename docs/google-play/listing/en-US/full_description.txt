You are a ranger of the Borderlands, and every run is a patrol on the edge of the kingdom. Ranger is a running journal that turns your runs into a story: creatures met on the way, a rank that grows, an outpost that is built up, and a map that shows only the land you have already run through.

RUNS FROM ANYWHERE
• Health Connect: Strava, Garmin, Samsung Health, Mi Fitness, Zepp, Polar and Coros write there. Runs come with their route and heart rate, plus your morning resting pulse, heart-rate variability and daily steps.
• GPX, TCX and FIT files from any watch, zipped or not.
• A whole Strava archive at once: years of runs, shoes included.
• Or record a run here with GPS, or type one in by hand.
When the same run arrives twice, one copy stays: the one with the track and the pulse.

THE BESTIARY
57 creatures live in the Borderlands, and the run itself decides who comes. The Nixie appears along water, the Moon Lynx in the dark, the Gold-horned Stag at sunrise. The Hill Troll waits on climbs, the Windchaser is caught only with surges, and the Whisperer shows herself to whoever runs quietly in the easy zones. Legends wait for the brave: the Frost Unicorn at a frozen dawn, the Elder Dragon at thirty kilometres, the Firebird on Midsummer night. Every creature is a watercolour portrait, first as tracks, then a lair, then the meeting.

RANKS, ABILITIES, CONDITION
• Valour is your training load: time weighted by how high the pulse was, so an easy hour and a hard half-hour count about the same.
• Ten ranks, from Recruit to Legend of the Borderlands. The painted outpost grows with each, from a campfire and tents to a stone keep with a golden flag, by day or by night as the real sky is.
• Nine abilities, each from its own kind of effort: endurance, speed, night eye, mountain path, foul-weather grit, quiet step and more.
• Condition from six-week fitness against one-week fatigue. When load climbs too fast or the morning pulse is up, the healer suggests a rest.

THE MAP AND THE CHRONICLE
• A parchment map from OpenStreetMap that reveals only what your trails have scouted, with seals where creatures were met. Tracks are cut around home and work.
• Campaigns over a few weeks: the Old Road, the High Pass, the Night Watch, the Quiet Step.
• 56 feats, each a medal.
• The chronicle: calendar, weekly kilometres, heart-rate zones, fitness and fatigue, resting pulse, steps, and best times for 1, 5 and 10 km, the half and the marathon.
• Painted cards of a patrol, a creature, a feat or a month, post or story shaped, to share anywhere.
• Shoe mileage, companions, and the bard: what played in your headphones and what you run faster to.

PRIVACY
Everything stays on your phone. No accounts, no ads, no analytics. Health Connect is only read, never written. Only map tiles (OpenFreeMap) and the weather (Open-Meteo, a rounded location) are requested over the internet. Free and open source.
