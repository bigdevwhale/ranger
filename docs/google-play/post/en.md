Ranger is now on Google Play 🏹

A running journal where every run is a patrol on the edge of the kingdom. Runs come in from Health Connect, your watch or a Strava archive, and the run itself decides who you meet: rain, fog, sunrise, a full moon, a river along the way.

• 57 creatures in watercolour, from the Nixie by the water to the Firebird on Midsummer night
• Ten ranks and a painted outpost that grows from a campfire to a stone keep
• A parchment map of only the land you have run through
• Load, condition, heart-rate zones and best times, plus cards to share

Free, no ads, no accounts. Everything stays on your phone.

Google Play: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=app.ranger
GitHub: https://github.com/bigdevwhale/ranger
